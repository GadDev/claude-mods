# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Welcome beginners, critique the work rather than the person, and make space for different experience levels.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, threats, and publishing private information are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, close discussions, or restrict participation to protect the community.

For a concern, contact the maintainer through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/GadDev). Do not post private incident details publicly. The maintainer will assess reports and choose a proportionate response.
